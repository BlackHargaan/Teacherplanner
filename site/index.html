Schedules that bend

Snow day? Bump everything forward in one click.

Lessons are kept in order for each class, so when a day is lost — a snow day, an assembly, a fire drill that ate your period — every later lesson slides to the next class meeting on its own. Extend a lesson that needs another day, move it, copy it to another class, or pull everything back.

  • Weekly, A/B week and rotating “Day 1, Day 2…” schedules
  • Half days, planning days and assemblies with their own bell times
  • Drag a lesson to a new day, or onto another class to copy it
  • Add an extra class or drop one from a single day
  • Reuse this year’s classes and lessons next year

Set up to ten sections for each class — or start from a familiar layout like 5E, Madeline Hunter, Gradual Release or Workshop. Attach videos, links, files and images. Tag lessons Draft, Ready or Taught so you always know where you stand.

  • Templates add your daily warm-up or exit ticket automatically
  • Strategies, differentiation and accommodations at a click
  • Colors, fonts and sizes for each class and section
  • Private notes only you can see

Standards

See what you’ve taught — and what you haven’t.

Tag lessons with standards and Teacher Planner keeps count. The coverage report shows every standard in a framework, how often you’ve taught it and which ones still need attention. Score students on standards with your own scale.

  • Starter sets from Common Core, NGSS and the C3 Framework
  • Add your state or district standards from a spreadsheet
  • Reports by class, framework and date range

Your files

Your plans are just files. On your computer.

When you first open Teacher Planner, you choose a folder. Everything you make is saved there as ordinary files — not on someone else’s server. If you ever stop using the app, your plans are still right there.

  • Works offline. No Wi-Fi in your classroom? No problem.
  • No sign-up. No account, no password to remember.
  • Automatic backups. A copy is saved every day, and the last 30 days are kept.
  • Home and school. Put your planner folder in OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox and open it on both computers.

Questions

Do I need the internet to use it?

No. Teacher Planner works completely offline. You only need the internet to download it, to get updates, and if you choose to sync your planner folder with OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox.

Where are my plans saved?

In the folder you pick the first time you open the app — usually Documents → Teacher Planner. Your classes, lessons and grades are in a file called planner.json, attachments are in the attachments folder, and a daily backup goes in backups. In the app, Settings → Data → Open folder takes you there.

Can I use it at school and at home?

Yes. Choose a folder inside OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox when you set it up, then install Teacher Planner on your other computer and choose the same folder. Changes show up on the other computer after they sync. Use one computer at a time so changes don’t overwrite each other.

Why does my computer warn me when I install it?

Windows and Mac show a warning for apps that haven’t been registered with Microsoft or Apple yet. It’s a one-time step — see the instructions under Download for your computer.

What if I deleted something by mistake?

Teacher Planner keeps a daily copy of your planner for the last 30 days. Close the app, open your planner folder, and copy a file from the backups folder over planner.json. You can also export a backup at any time from Settings → Data.

Can my students or a substitute see my plans?

Yes. Under Shared Views you decide exactly what each audience sees — which classes, which lesson sections, which days — and open it on a classroom screen or save it as a web page to email or post.

How do I get the newest version?

Teacher Planner checks for new versions on its own. On Windows and Linux it downloads them in the background and shows a Restart to update button. On Mac it shows a Download button that brings you to this page, and you replace the app in Applications. Your plans stay safely in your planner folder either way. To check yourself, go to Settings → Updates or Help → Check for Updates.

Does it work on a Chromebook, iPad or phone?

Not yet. Teacher Planner runs on Windows, Mac and Linux computers.
